## Prompt utilizado

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: transparent PNG character cutout for the Clara Pro login page. Image 1 is the existing fictional 3D Clara character and is the identity/style/clothing reference. Image 2 is ONLY the pose/composition reference; do not use the real woman's identity or clothing. Create a new pose of the SAME 3D Clara from Image 1: preserve her friendly face, warm brown skin, expressive brown eyes, shoulder-length wavy dark-brown hair, gold hoop earrings and small round pendant, burgundy blazer and ivory top. She smiles warmly at the viewer, leaning slightly forward with one forearm resting horizontally as if over the top of an invisible board, while her other arm comes forward and its index finger points clearly downward toward the headline that will be placed below her on a webpage, just like the pose in Image 2. Remove the notebook. Show complete head, hair, shoulders, both elbows and hands, and only upper torso; waist and legs not needed. Natural anatomically coherent hands, one clear pointing index finger. Polished friendly 3D rendering consistent with Image 1, soft studio light. Tightly framed landscape composition with small breathing space around silhouette. Background must be genuinely transparent with alpha, no checkerboard rendered, no backdrop, no white rectangle, no board, no table, no typography, no logos, no shadows outside the character. The pointing hand is the bottommost part of the silhouette, directed down toward the space below the asset. Output only the character asset, not a website mockup.
