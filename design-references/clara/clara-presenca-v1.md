Preserve exactly her face and identity, warm brown skin, expressive brown eyes, wavy shoulder-length dark brown hair, burgundy blazer, ivory blouse, gold hoop earrings and round pendant. New welcoming pose for empty states in her scheduling app: waist-up, smiling directly at viewer, holding a small ivory client-record clipboard with a burgundy clip in one arm, the other hand gently presenting the clipboard with an open palm. Clipboard contains only very simple abstract thin lines and one small calendar grid, no readable words or letters. Complete head and hands, relaxed natural anatomy, softly lit same 3D style as reference. Character fills frame with small breathing space, neat waist crop. Genuinely transparent alpha background, no backdrop, no checkerboard, no background shadow, no text or watermark. Return only the cutout character, not an interface.
